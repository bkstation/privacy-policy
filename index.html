1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications (“Apps”) developed and published by BK Station (“we,” “our,” or “us”), available on Google Play, the App Store and other platforms. This policy explains what information our Apps may collect and how it is used.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on an App's features and the third-party services it uses, our Apps may collect some of the following types of information. Not every BK Station App collects every category listed below.

  • Device information, such as device model, operating-system version and related technical information.
  • Advertising identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID, used for advertising and related services where permitted.
  • Randomized app or device identifiers used for analytics, diagnostics and distinguishing installations or sessions.
  • App usage data and diagnostics, such as crash reports, performance data and technical errors.
  • App interactions and gameplay information, such as sessions, progress, feature usage and in-app actions.
  • Approximate location, if required for features such as ad personalization; this does not mean precise GPS location.

The information collected depends on the functionality of each App and the third-party services integrated with it. We do not collect names, addresses or other directly identifying personal information unless an App explicitly requests such information for a specific feature, in which case this policy may be updated as appropriate.

Additional restrictions apply to child-directed Apps and child users, as explained in the Children's Privacy section below.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve our Apps.
  • To understand gameplay, feature usage, app interactions and user engagement.
  • To display advertisements through third-party ad networks, such as Google AdMob, in Apps that include advertising.
  • To analyze app performance and identify and fix crashes, bugs and technical issues.
★

4. Children's Privacy

Some BK Station Apps may be designed for or suitable for children, while others are intended for a broader audience. We identify the intended age groups for each App in the relevant app-store listing.

For child-directed Apps, and for children or users of unknown age in mixed-audience Apps where applicable:

  • We do not use personalized or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not transmit prohibited persistent device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID.
  • If advertising is included, we use services and configurations permitted for child-directed use and require age-appropriate ads.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent where consent is required.
  • A mixed-audience App may use a neutral age screen when needed to apply the appropriate protections.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and, where required, remove it.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and improve our Apps, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect our rights. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the third-party service provider involved.

You may request access to or deletion of information associated with you by contacting us using the email address below. We will handle requests as reasonably applicable based on the information available to us.

Some information may be retained where required by law, for security and fraud prevention, to protect our rights or for necessary record-keeping purposes. Information processed by third-party service providers is also subject to those providers' own retention and deletion policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information collected through our Apps, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
